# Scroll

Makes a container horizontally scrollable on small screens.
 
	<div class="b-scroll" style="max-width: 400px;">
		<div class="b-scroll__content">
			<table class="c-table c-table--border-rows">
				<thead>
					<tr>
						<th>Head 1</th>
						<th>Head 2</th>
						<th>Head 3</th>
						<th>Head 4</th>
						<th>Head 5</th>
						<th>Head 6</th>
					</tr>
				</thead>
				<tbody>
					<tr>
						<td>Cell 1</td>
						<td>Cell 2</td>
						<td>Cell 3</td>
						<td>Cell 4</td>
						<td>Cell 5</td>
						<td>Cell 6</td>
					</tr>
					<tr>
						<td>Cell 1</td>
						<td>Cell 2</td>
						<td>Cell 3</td>
						<td>Cell 4</td>
						<td>Cell 5</td>
						<td>Cell 6</td>
					</tr>
				</tbody>
			</table>
		</div>
	</div>
