# Box

Creates a small inline label for status, count, or metadata.
  
	<div class="c-box u-color-neutral">	
		Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. 
	</div>

<div class="u-vite-preview">
	<div class="c-box u-color-neutral">
		Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. 
	</div>
</div> 
  
## Modifiers

### Spacing

Applies padding on the block and inline axis.

		Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. 
	</div>

Applies a thin border around the box.

	<div class="c-box c-box--bordered u-color-neutral">	
		Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.
